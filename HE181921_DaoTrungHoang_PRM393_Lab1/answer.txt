Design a modern personal finance management app UI inspired by YNAB.
Style: clean, minimal, fintech-grade, trust-focused.
Color palette: soft neutral background, strong accent color for actions, red only for overspending alerts.
Typography: modern sans-serif, high readability.
Layout: card-based, clear hierarchy, no clutter.
Tone: calm, guiding, non-judgmental.

Principles:
- Separate money storage (accounts) from spending intent (categories).
- Show "Available" amounts clearly.
- Encourage intentional decisions, not passive tracking.
- Mobile-first, responsive for web and desktop.

Screen 1 – Goal Definition
Design a questionnaire screen asking about the user's main financial goal.
Question text:
"What do you want your money to help you with right now?"

Answer options as large selectable cards:
- Build an emergency fund
- Control monthly spending
- Save for a big purchase
- Grow wealth long-term

Style:
Warm, welcoming, not sales-driven.
Illustrated icons for each option.
Screen 2 – Time Horizon
Design a questionnaire screen asking about time horizon.
Question text:
"When do you need this money?"

Answer options:
- Less than 1 year
- 1–3 years
- 3–5 years
- More than 5 years

Use a slider or pill-style buttons.
Emphasize clarity and simplicity.
Screen 3 – Risk Tolerance (Behavior-based)
Design a questionnaire screen assessing risk tolerance.
Question text:
"If your plan temporarily dropped by 20%, what would you most likely do?"

Answer options:
- Sell immediately
- Wait and see
- Stay the course
- Invest more

Tone: calm, non-judgmental.
No red or green emotional cues.
Screen 4 – Financial Safety Check
Design a questionnaire screen about financial safety.
Question text:
"How many months of essential expenses could you cover today?"

Answer options:
- Less than 1 month
- 1–3 months
- 3–6 months
- More than 6 months

Include a subtle helper text explaining why this matters.
Screen 5 – Summary Screen
Design a summary screen showing the user's financial profile.
Display:
- Profile type (e.g., Balanced)
- Key traits (short-term horizon, moderate risk tolerance)
- Friendly reassurance message

Include a primary button:
"Generate my plan"

Style:
Confidence-building, not authoritative.
Screen 6 – Allocation Recommendation UI
Design a recommendation screen for budget and allocation planning.
Show one recommended allocation card (e.g., 20 / 60 / 20).
Explain each part in plain language.
Include:
- Why this plan fits the user
- What problem it solves
- What trade-offs exist

Actions:
- Apply plan
- Customize manually

Avoid financial jargon and investment promises.
Screen 7 – Reassign Allocation (YNAB-style)
Design a budget reallocation screen.
Show categories with "Available" amounts.
Allow drag-and-drop or numeric input to move money between categories.
Real-time update of balances.
Clear warning when a category becomes overspent.

Language:
"Move money from" instead of "Transfer funds".
